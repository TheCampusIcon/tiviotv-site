FAQ

Questions

What do I need?

A TV with an HDMI port and a Wi-Fi connection. We send the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus. Tivio works on the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus and 4K Max only.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Send us a friend who signs up and your next month is free.

How does setup work?

Once your stick arrives, call our support line at (762) 218-9309 or email support@tiviotv.com. We stay on the phone with you until everything is working.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

How much does it cost?

Right now our first customers get the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus and phone setup free, with no setup fee. You just pay $40 a month for ongoing support. Any streaming subscriptions you choose are billed by those services.

Can I use my own apps?

Yes. It is your Fire TV Stick. Use YouTube, free live TV apps or any service you subscribe to. We help you set them up and keep them running.

Can I take it with me?

Yes. Plug it into any TV with Wi-Fi, at home, on vacation or at a friend's place. Some apps limit what is available outside the US.
